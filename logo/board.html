What we heard

The brief

An app lock for Finnish learners: distracting apps stay locked each day until the study goal is met. It should feel disciplined but friendly, warm, and unmistakably Finnish.

disciplinedfriendlywarmFinnish

Success: reads as both 'ä' and 'lock' at a glance, holds up at 16 px, works in one colour, stands apart from blue study apps.

Final design

Umlaut shackle

The umlaut dots join into a shackle: ä becomes a lock.

  • One shape carries both meanings of the name: sana (word, the ä) and lukko (lock)
  • Coral shackle = the part that opens when today's goal is met
  • Cream and coral stand out on a home screen full of blue study apps
